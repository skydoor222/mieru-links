Privacy Policy — reel-loop

Last updated: 2026-11-13

reel-loop is a private tool used by a single owner to post short videos that the owner made to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. It has no other users.

What we access

When the owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit, reel-loop receives an access token for the owner's own account with these scopes:

  • user.info.basic — to confirm which of the owner's accounts is connected
  • video.publish — to post the owner's own videos
  • video.list — to read view, like, comment and share counts of the owner's own posted videos

What we store

Only the post ID and the counts above, for the owner's own videos, in the owner's private repository. Tokens are stored encrypted. No data about other TikTok users is read or stored.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Nothing is shown to anyone other than the owner.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings > Security > Apps and services). Stored counts are deleted on request.

YouTube API Services

reel-loop uses YouTube API Services to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels and to read the view, like and comment counts of those videos. By using reel-loop you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's Privacy Policy applies to data handled by YouTube.

  • Data accessed: channel ID and title (to confirm which channel is connected), and, for videos uploaded by reel-loop only, the video ID and view/like/comment counts.
  • Use: to upload the owner's videos and to compare how the owner's own videos performed. Nothing is shared with third parties or shown to anyone other than the owner.
  • Storage: video IDs and counts are kept in the owner's private repository until the owner deletes them. The refresh token is stored encrypted and is deleted when access is revoked.
  • Revoking access: Google security settings (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Stored data is deleted on request through the contact below.
  • No cookies or similar technologies are placed on any user's device by reel-loop.

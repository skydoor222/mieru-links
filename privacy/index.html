Privacy Policy — reel-loop

Last updated: 2026-11-12

reel-loop is a private tool used by a single owner to post short videos that the owner made to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. It has no other users.

What we access

When the owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit, reel-loop receives an access token for the owner's own account with these scopes:

  • user.info.basic — to confirm which of the owner's accounts is connected
  • video.publish — to post the owner's own videos
  • video.list — to read view, like, comment and share counts of the owner's own posted videos

What we store

Only the post ID and the counts above, for the owner's own videos, in the owner's private repository. Tokens are stored encrypted. No data about other TikTok users is read or stored.

Sharing

Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Nothing is shown to anyone other than the owner.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings > Security > Apps and services). Stored counts are deleted on request.
